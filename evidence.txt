**COMPANY / RESULTS**  
- Q1 FY27 revenue reported as **₹1,428 crore**, a 35 % YoY increase; net profit **₹82 crore** (≈6 % rise) and operating margin **11.2 %** (down 170 bps)【S1】.  
- In the company‑issued press release, Q1 FY27 revenue is **₹1,248 crore**, an 18 % YoY increase; EBITDA **₹140 crore** (2.6 % rise) with margin **11.2 %** (‑170 bps); profit after tax **₹82 crore** (5.9 % rise)【S3】.  
- Order book stood at **₹3,900 crore** as of 30 Jun 2026, up from **₹3,150 crore** a year earlier【S3】.  
- Exports contributed **21 %** of Q1 revenue (up from 16 % in Q1 FY26)【S3】.  
- Shares fell **3.2 %** to ₹1,012 on the NSE after the results announcement【S1】.  

**GROWTH**  
- Management maintains FY27 full‑year revenue growth guidance of **15‑17 %**【S2】.  
- Data‑centre cable sales now represent **≈9 %** of domestic revenue and are expected to **double in two years**【S2】.  
- New Bharuch (Gujarat) plant adds about **30 %** to cable capacity and is slated for commercial production in **Q3 FY27**【S3】.  
- Bharuch plant capex is **₹620 crore** total, with ~70 % already spent【S2】.  
- Receivable days rose from **78 to 96** over the last year【S2】.  

**RISKS**  
- Copper prices rose **≈14 %** in the quarter, compressing margins by 170 bps【S1】; management attributes most margin pressure to copper【S2】.  
- About **two‑thirds** of contracts have price‑variation clauses that reset with a **1‑2 quarter lag**, exposing the firm to cost‑pass‑through risk【S2】.  
- Promoter‑pledge level reported as **35 %** of holdings (up from 22 %) raises governance concerns【S4】.  
- GST demand order of **₹46.3 crore** (including interest/penalty) for FY2020‑21 & FY2021‑22 input‑tax credit; company expects no material impact【S7】.  
- Increase in receivable days to 96 days may affect cash flow【S2】.  

**OWNERSHIP**  
- Shareholding pattern (30 Jun 2026): Promoters **58.2 %**, of which **4.1 %** is pledged; FIIs **11.8 %**, DIIs **14.6 %**, public **15.4 %**【S8】.
