**## Snapshot**  
- **Q1 FY27 revenue:** reported as **₹1,428 crore** (35 % YoY) in one source【S1】 and **₹1,248 crore** (18 % YoY) in the company press release【S3】.  
- **Net profit / PAT:** ₹82 crore, ~6 % rise YoY【S1】 (company press release confirms the same figure【S3】).  
- **Operating margin:** 11.2 % (down 170 bps)【S1】【S3】.  
- **Order book:** ₹3,900 crore as of 30 Jun 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier【S3】.  
- **Export contribution:** 21 % of Q1 revenue, up from 16 % in Q1 FY26【S3】.  
- **Share price reaction:** fell 3.2 % to ₹1,012 on the NSE after results announcement【S1】.  

**## Bull case**  
- Management maintains FY27 full‑year revenue growth guidance of **15‑17 %**【S2】, supported by a strong order book and expanding export share.  
- **Data‑centre cable** sales now represent ~9 % of domestic revenue and are projected to **double in two years**【S2】, offering a higher‑margin growth avenue.  
- New **Bharuch (Gujarat) plant** adds ~30 % to cable capacity and is slated for commercial production in **Q3 FY27**【S3】; capex of **₹620 crore** is ~70 % spent【S2】, indicating near‑term capacity uplift.  
- Order book growth (≈24 % YoY) provides visibility for future sales【S3】.  
- Export share rising to 21 % suggests diversification beyond the domestic market【S3】.  

**## Bear case**  
- **Copper price surge (~14 %)** compressed margins by 170 bps in the quarter【S1】; management attributes most margin pressure to copper【S2】.  
- **Price‑variation clauses** on ~2/3 of contracts reset with a 1‑2 quarter lag, limiting the firm’s ability to pass higher input costs to customers【S2】.  
- **Receivable days** increased from 78 to 96, potentially straining cash flow【S2】.  
- **Promoter‑pledge concerns:** one source claims **35 %** of promoter holdings are pledged【S4】, while the official shareholding pattern shows only **4.1 %** pledged【S8】—raising governance uncertainty.  
- Share price already reacted negatively (‑3.2 %) post‑results【S1】.  

**## Open questions**
